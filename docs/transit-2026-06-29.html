HIGH LA Metro LA City

Vermont Avenue bus project moves ahead without bike lanes after judge's ruling

June 15 – June 25, 2026

A Los Angeles Superior Court judge's June 15 preliminary ruling, reported June 25, allows Metro's $400 million Vermont Avenue bus rapid transit project to proceed without the protected bike lanes that street-safety advocate Joe Linton argues are legally required under Measure HLA. The ruling is a preliminary decision on an injunction tied to Linton's broader lawsuit, which contends the city's role in permitting and design review obligates it to implement Mobility Plan upgrades, including bike lanes, along the corridor.

Key Facts

  • $400 million project adds dedicated bus lanes along more than 12 miles of Vermont Avenue between 120th Street and Sunset Boulevard
  • Vermont Avenue has among the highest rates of pedestrian deaths and injuries of any corridor in the city
  • Lawsuit filed in April 2025 by Joe Linton, editor of Streetsblog LA, suing as a resident, not in his publication role
  • Metro and the LA City Attorney argue Measure HLA's Mobility Plan upgrades apply only to projects the city itself leads, not Metro's

Why it matters: The ruling tests whether Metro-led capital projects can sidestep city-mandated street-safety upgrades by routing permitting through the county instead of the city — a precedent that could shape how other regional megaprojects handle bike and pedestrian infrastructure going forward.

It's a preview of the kind of jurisdictional disputes likely to recur as more transit capital projects move through overlapping city, county, and regional approval processes.

Regional transit agencies are diverging sharply on fiscal footing

San Diego MTS approved a $482.5 million FY27 budget with no service cuts and is weighing new affordability protections for low-income riders — a notably different trajectory than Metrolink's indefinite 20% service cuts further north. The split illustrates how unevenly post-pandemic recovery and cost pressures are landing across SoCal's patchwork of transit operators.
