AMOC Cold Blob Signals Possible Climate Tipping
Scientists are warning that a growing cold blob of anomalously cold water south of Greenland in the North Atlantic may indicate the AMOC ocean circulation system is weakening. A collapse of AMOC would dramatically alter weather patterns across Europe and North America.
  • Cold blob spans approximately 1,000+ km in the North Atlantic south of Greenland
  • AMOC has weakened ~15% since the mid-20th century per prior studies
  • Collapse could lower European temperatures by 5–10°C over decades
AMOC collapse is considered an irreversible climate tipping point that would devastate agriculture and coastal regions across two continents.
