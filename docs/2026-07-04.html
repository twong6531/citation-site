CO2 in Meeting Rooms Kills Decision Quality

Research shows that CO2 concentrations in closed meeting rooms routinely reach 2,000+ ppm, causing measurable cognitive impairment. Lawrence Berkeley National Lab studies found that at 2,500 ppm, 7 of 9 decision-making measures fall into the "dysfunctional" range.

  • At 1,000 ppm CO2, performance drops on 6 of 9 decision metrics vs a 600 ppm baseline
  • A closed room with a few people reaches 1,000 ppm within the first hour
  • One real-world reading recorded 2,143 ppm — nearly 5× outdoor CO2 levels (~400 ppm)
Every high-stakes meeting — strategy reviews, architecture sessions — takes place in exactly the conditions that impair the cognition needed to run them.
